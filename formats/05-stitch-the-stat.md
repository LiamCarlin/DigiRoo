# 05 · Stitch / react to the stat

**Pillar:** B · **Length:** 15–25 s · **Hook type:** Pattern interrupt / reaction

**Use when:** A news clip, study or creator video about kids' screen time or tech neck is circulating.

## Structure (beats)
[0–4 s] Stitch or greenscreen the headline/stat (screenshot is fine). [4–12 s] Antonia reacts: 'Here's what nobody says next.' [12–22 s] The practical bit: screen height, demo. CTA.

## Script skeleton
```
'Okay, so this stat is everywhere this week.' [stat on screen]
'Here's the part nobody says next: you can't un-screen a kid. But you can move the screen.'
'This is what we do.' [demo]
'DigiRoo. Bio.'
```

## Shot list skeleton (phone only)
1. Greenscreen/stitch of the article headline, 4 s
2. Antonia to camera, 8 s
3. Demo: slouch → lift, 8 s

## On-screen text pattern
headline screenshot / 'you can't un-screen a kid' / 'but you can move the screen'

## Why it works
Rides existing attention; positions DigiRoo as the practical answer, not the scold.

## How DigiRoo uses it
Only cite the stat as the source states it (title + outlet on screen). Don't extrapolate to kids if the study was adults.

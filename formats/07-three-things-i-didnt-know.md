# 07 · Three things I didn't know until I became a mom

**Pillar:** A · **Length:** 25–35 s · **Hook type:** Listicle / curiosity gap

**Use when:** You have talking-head energy and no kid available.

## Structure (beats)
[0–2 s] 'Three things nobody told me about kids and screens.' [2–10 s] #1 (relatable, not product). [10–18 s] #2 (relatable). [18–30 s] #3 = screen height; demo. CTA.

## Script skeleton
```
'Three things nobody told me about kids and tablets.'
'One: they will find the iPad. Doesn't matter where you hid it.'
'Two: the volume will always be at 100.'
'Three: the real problem isn't how long, it's how low. Head down, neck down, for an hour.'
'So I made a case that lifts it. DigiRoo. In my bio.'
```

## Shot list skeleton (phone only)
1. Antonia selfie to camera, 3 beats
2. Cutaway for each point (iPad hiding spot, volume, slouch), 3 s each
3. Demo lift, 5 s

## On-screen text pattern
'1' '2' '3' / 'not how long, how low'

## Why it works
Numbered structure holds attention; two non-product beats earn the third.

## How DigiRoo uses it
Keep points 1–2 funny and universal; point 3 is always the product.

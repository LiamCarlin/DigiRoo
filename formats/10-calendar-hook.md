# 10 · Awareness-day hook (World Spine Day etc.)

**Pillar:** D · **Length:** 15–30 s · **Hook type:** Timely / occasion

**Use when:** An awareness day or season is within 3 days: World Spine Day (Oct 16), back-to-school, Screen-Free Week, gift guides, road-trip season.

## Structure (beats)
[0–2 s] 'It's World Spine Day and my kid is folded in half over Bluey.' [2–12 s] One honest beat about the day/season. [12–25 s] The small thing we do (demo). CTA tied to the day.

## Script skeleton
```
'It's World Spine Day, and this is what my six-year-old's spine is doing right now.' [slouch]
'I'm not going to fight the screen. I'm going to raise it.' [lift]
'That's it. That's the post. DigiRoo, bio.'
```

## Shot list skeleton (phone only)
1. Kid slouched + day label, 4 s
2. Antonia to camera, 6 s
3. Lift demo, 8 s

## On-screen text pattern
'World Spine Day 🦴' / 'raise the screen, not your voice'

## Why it works
Hashtag and search spike on the day; clinics repost; low effort because the structure is fixed.

## How DigiRoo uses it
Check research/awareness-calendar.md every content-week run. Schedule the post for the morning of the day.

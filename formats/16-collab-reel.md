# 16 · Collab reel (co-posted with a partner)

**Pillar:** D · **Length:** 15–45 s · **Hook type:** Borrowed audience / guest face

**Use when:** A parent creator, school, clinic, event partner or local business will co-post. On Instagram use the Collab invite when posting, so the reel lives on both profiles and the counts merge. On TikTok there's no equivalent: the partner posts and tags @digiroocase, or we stitch them.

## Structure (beats)
[0–2 s] The guest's face and a joint hook: 'I'm Caroline, a pediatric physio, and Antonia asked me one question.' [2–20 s] The guest's bit: their tip, story or event, in their voice. [20–35 s] Antonia (or the product) answers with one thing parents can do; the stand lifts on screen. [35–45 s] The ask: event date, or 'follow us both'.

## Script skeleton
```
GUEST:   'I'm [name], [who they are]. Antonia asked me [question].'
GUEST:   [their answer / tip / event in one breath]
ANTONIA: 'Which is why the screen comes up to eye level in our house.' [stand lifts]
BOTH:    'Follow us both for the next one.' / 'See you on [date].'
```

## Shot list skeleton (phone only)
1. Guest to camera, natural light, their own space, 5–15 s
2. Antonia to camera or a whip-pan between the two, 5 s
3. Kid + DigiRoo stand lifting, 3–5 s
4. Both in frame, or the event card, 3 s

## On-screen text pattern
Word-by-word captions, white boxes, centre. The guest's name and role on screen in the first 2 s.

## Why it works
The partner's followers see it as their own feed content, not an ad, and collab reels count reach from both audiences. A second credible face (clinician, educator, parent creator) carries trust that a solo product post can't.

## How DigiRoo uses it
Plan one a month. Partners in order: Bermuda event/community partners, affiliate clinics (pairs with format 14), parent creators with kids aged 4–12. Get written OK before filming. Clinician collabs get **[REVIEW: clinician]** on the post card, and no clinician endorsement until it's agreed in writing (brief §5). Kids on camera need their parent's written OK.

# 12 · 'But will my kid actually use it?' (objection kill)

**Pillar:** C · **Length:** 20–30 s · **Hook type:** Direct address / objection

**Use when:** Comments or DMs raise the same doubt (durability, will they use it, does it fit, is it worth $50).

## Structure (beats)
[0–2 s] Read the objection on screen. [2–15 s] Show the answer, don't argue it: drop test, kid carrying it, fits check, 3 months of use. [15–25 s] One honest caveat. CTA.

## Script skeleton
```
'Someone asked: will my kid actually use the stand or just ignore it?'
'Fair. Here's day one.' [kid ignores it]
'Here's week two.' [kid flips it up without being asked]
'Turns out eye level is just comfier. They figure it out.'
'DigiRoo, bio.'
```

## Shot list skeleton (phone only)
1. Screenshot of the comment, 3 s
2. Proof clips, 10 s
3. Antonia caveat to camera, 5 s

## On-screen text pattern
the objection verbatim / 'day 1' 'week 2'

## Why it works
Answers the question the buyer is silently asking; comment screenshots read as social proof.

## How DigiRoo uses it
Keep a running list of objections in lessons-learned.md; one per month becomes this format.

# 03 · Chiropractor explains tech neck in 20 s

**Pillar:** B · **Length:** 20–30 s · **Hook type:** Credential + problem callout

**Use when:** Pillar B week; you have a clinician quote (or an affiliate willing to be on camera / voice note).

## Structure (beats)
[0–2 s] 'A pediatric chiropractor told me the one thing she wishes parents knew about tablets.' [2–10 s] The point, in plain words: the lower the screen, the further the head drops, the more load on the neck. Show the angle with a hand gesture or a kid profile. [10–20 s] 'The habit fix': bring the screen up. Demo. [20–28 s] 'Not medical advice, just the thing that worked at our house.' CTA.

## Script skeleton
```
'A pediatric chiropractor told me the one thing she wishes parents knew about tablets.'
'It's not the screen time. It's the screen height.'
'When the screen's in their lap, the head goes down and stays down.'
'Bring the screen up to eye level and the neck comes with it.'
'That's the whole reason I made DigiRoo. Link's in the bio.'
```

## Shot list skeleton (phone only)
1. Antonia to camera, selfie, 6 s
2. Kid profile slouched, 4 s
3. Hand gesture showing head angle OR drawn arrow overlay, 4 s
4. Stand raised, kid upright, 6 s
5. Antonia to camera for CTA, 4 s

## On-screen text pattern
'it's not the screen time… it's the screen height' / 'eye level' / [REVIEW: clinician]

## Why it works
Borrowed authority without medical claims; keyword-dense (tech neck, pediatric chiropractor, screen time) for search; clinicians share it because it makes them look good.

## How DigiRoo uses it
Always flag for review. Never name a clinician who hasn't agreed. Reuse as the first post in the affiliate content pack.

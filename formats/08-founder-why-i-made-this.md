# 08 · Why I made this (founder story)

**Pillar:** E · **Length:** 30–45 s · **Hook type:** Confession / origin

**Use when:** Trust-building, new-follower weeks, Bermuda press moments, awards anniversaries.

## Structure (beats)
[0–2 s] 'I made this for my daughter. Then 1,200 other families bought it.' [2–15 s] Lockdown 2020, saw her folded over the iPad, sketched it on 17 Aug 2020. [15–30 s] Prototype → patent → Fast Company → shelves in Bermuda. Show the thing. [30–40 s] 'Now I'm trying to get it to more families. Follow along.'

## Script skeleton
```
'I made this for my daughter. Then about twelve hundred other families bought it.'
'Lockdown, 2020. She was folded in half over an iPad and I couldn't un-see it.'
'I sketched a case with a stand that lifts the screen. Then I actually built it.'
'Patent, Fast Company honourable mention, Bermuda shop shelves.'
'Now I want it in more homes. That's what this account is.'
```

## Shot list skeleton (phone only)
1. Antonia to camera, 10 s
2. Old sketch / prototype photos, 5 s
3. Product hero, 5 s
4. Daughter using it today, 8 s
5. Bermuda exterior / packing an order, 5 s

## On-screen text pattern
'2020' / '1,200 families' / 'Fast Company 2022' / 'Bermuda 🇧🇲'

## Why it works
Founder faces outperform brand voice for small accounts; specific numbers and dates read as true.

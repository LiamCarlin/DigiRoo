# 11 · Trending sound + slouch-to-lift reveal

**Pillar:** D · **Length:** 7–15 s · **Hook type:** Trend / transition

**Use when:** A sound is trending with a 'transition' or 'reveal' beat and you have 10 minutes.

## Structure (beats)
[0–beat] Slouch shot timed to the build. [beat] Cut on the drop: stand up, kid up. [after] Hold, text CTA.

## Script skeleton
```
none; text only: 'tablet neck' → 'tablet neck, elevated'
```

## Shot list skeleton (phone only)
1. Slouch, static, 4–6 s
2. Lift, same framing, 4–6 s

## On-screen text pattern
one word before, one word after

## Why it works
Reach from the sound; the product moment is the reveal, so it never feels like an ad.

## How DigiRoo uses it
Pick the sound in-app at post time (sounds don't survive cross-posting; re-add on IG).

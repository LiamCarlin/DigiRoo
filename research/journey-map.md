# Journey maps (D11)

## A. Worried Mom — from scroll to purchase to advocate

| Stage | Trigger / touchpoint | What she does | What she feels | Our content job | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Notice | TikTok FYP / Reels: POV slouch spot (format 01) or before/after (02) | Watches 3–7 s, maybe likes | "That's my kid." | Hook in 1.5 s, side-profile proof | 3-s retention, views |
| 2. Consider | Sees us 2–3 more times: chiro explainer (03), unboxing (04), stitch-the-stat (05) | Saves, sends to partner, visits profile | "Is this a real thing? Is it worth $50?" | Trust: founder story (08), clinician voice, objection kill (12) | saves, shares, profile visits |
| 3. Check | Bio link → kidsdigitalwellness.com; maybe Amazon/Walmart search | Checks iPad fit, price, shipping, reviews | "Does it fit the 10th gen? Shipping to Bermuda/US?" | Clear fit table on PDP; FAQ post | link clicks, PDP views |
| 4. Buy | Shopify checkout (affiliate code if she came via a clinic) | Orders | Relief, "did a good-mom thing" | Thank-you email with UGC brief + code to share | orders, code uses |
| 5. Use | Unboxing with kid | Kid finds the stand; maybe ignores it day 1 | Mild doubt → "oh, they just use it now" | Day-1/week-2 expectation setting (12) | returns, support DMs |
| 6. Advocate | Prompted by email/DM | Posts an unboxing or tags us | Pride | UGC brief = format 04 shot list; repost via 13 | UGC clips received, tags |

**Biggest leak today:** stage 1→2. Posts stop at ~500 views so she never sees us 3 times. Fix = cadence + hooks (H1).

## B. Pediatric Chiro / PT — from DM to posting affiliate

| Stage | Touchpoint | What they do | What they feel | Our job | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Contact | Personalised IG DM or email (templates/outreach-*) | Reads, maybe checks our profile | "Is this spam?" | Specific reference to their content; no link in first DM; founder voice | reply rate |
| 2. Evaluate | One-pager + our profile | Looks for claims, credibility, effort required | "Will this make me look salesy?" | No medical claims; Fast Company/patent; "zero admin" | yes rate |
| 3. Onboard | Unit shipped + code + content pack | Receives, tries in clinic | "Oh, this is actually decent." | Fast shipping, personal note, 3 ready posts | days to onboard |
| 4. Activate | Posts content / hands out code card | First repost | "Easy win for my feed." | Tag + repost them; thank publicly | posts by affiliates |
| 5. Convert | Patient uses code | First sale | "It works." | Monthly note with their numbers | code uses, commission paid |
| 6. Retain | Monthly content drop | Keeps posting | Partner, not vendor | New formats quarterly; feature them (format 03) | active affiliates after 90 days |

**Biggest leak today:** stage 3→5. 1–2 onboarded, no sales: no content pack, no code card, no follow-up. Fix = content pack + monthly loop (H3).

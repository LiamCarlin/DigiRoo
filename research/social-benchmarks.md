## 1. What is working right now (patterns across the accounts below)

1. **Practitioner authority + one simple fix.** The biggest posture clips are from PTs showing a single movement ("The only move you need to fix forward posture") with a hook in the first second. Dr. Rod (@drrodpt) ~1.1M likes on a "Fix Your Posture" clip; Dr. Josh PT (@drjoshpt) ~30K–757K likes on posture clips; Dr. Dan DPT (@dr.dan_dpt) ~281K likes on "posture correctors make you weaker" ([TikTok discover: posture corrector](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/posture-corrector); [@dr.dan_dpt](https://www.tiktok.com/@dr.dan_dpt/video/7246149552154152238?lang=en); [@drjoshpt](https://www.tiktok.com/@drjoshpt/video/7602808579866823950)).
2. **"Tummy time for adults" is the dominant tech-neck meme** (one clip ~209K likes; PT clinics calling it "a 5-minute fix for tech neck") — a kid-friendly reversal ("your toddler already knows the fix") is an open lane ([TikTok discover: tummy time tech neck](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/tummy-time-tech-neck)).
3. **Screen-time rules content triggers debate, and debate drives distribution.** Kayla (@kayla23ma) posted her tablet rules and "had TikTokers debating whether she's too strict" ([AOL/In The Know](https://www.aol.com/lifestyle/mom-tablet-rules-spark-controversy-161549647.html)). A researcher's "how I instantly know a child is an iPad kid" clip passed 1.2M views ([Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/news/ipad-kids/)). SCMP notes "an entire subset of TikTok videos – racking up millions of views – where users call out and mock iPad kids" ([SCMP](https://mcdn.i-scmp.com/lifestyle/health-wellness/article/3242581/why-gen-z-fear-they-will-raise-generation-terribly-behaved-ipad-kids-stoking-tiktok-debate-about)).
4. **Tutorial/how-to "mom tech" content is searchable and evergreen.** @momtestedfinds' "Setting up a kid's iPad from scratch the RIGHT way" and YouTube Kids time-limit tutorials are stuffed with search keywords and tied to Christmas gifting ([TikTok @momtestedfinds](https://www.tiktok.com/@momtestedfinds/video/7585663679555816735)). TikTok now functions as a search engine; captions written like search queries get surfaced.
5. **Before/after and X-ray visuals.** Chiropractic clinics post "mid-back rounding" X-rays and posture before/afters under #kidsposture ([TikTok discover](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/posture-before-and-after-chiropractor)). Controversial for infants (doctors object, [AOL](https://www.aol.com/news/baby-chiropractors-rack-views-moms-231345931.html)), but the visual format (side-profile silhouette, angle overlay) transfers cleanly to a product demo.
6. **Pediatric PTs post practical, low-risk steps**: "keep devices near eye level, prop tablets up, take movement breaks, lie on the stomach" ([TikTok discover: kids posture chiropractor](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/chiropractor-kid)). DigiRoo is literally the "prop tablets up" step.
7. **Carousels still out-engage Reels per person reached on Instagram** (Buffer data cited in [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/Instagram-reels-stats)); Metricool's 2026 Instagram study says "Reels and carousels are reshaping platform strategy" ([Metricool press release](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/)). Repurpose each video as a 5-slide carousel ("5 signs your kid has tech neck").

## 3. Platform benchmarks (each number with its source)

### TikTok views for small accounts
- **Median 378 views per video for accounts under 10K followers**; 560 for 10K–100K; 2,771 for 100K–1M ([Heepsy](https://www.heepsy.com/api/free-tools/tiktok-average-views-calculator)). DigiRoo's ~500 views is slightly above this median.
- Creators under 10K followers reach **about half their follower count per video** (median; 6,647 videos, 230 creators, Oct 2026) ([SociaVault](https://sociavault.com/blog/tiktok-benchmarks-2026)).
- **Only ~1 in 6 matured TikTok posts clears 1,000 views** (508K posts, 1,900 creators) ([Quso.ai](https://quso.ai/research/tiktok-data-findings)).
- **76% of TikTok creators get fewer than 1K views per post** (secondary report, methodology not shown) ([Digital Information World, Mar 2026](https://www.digitalinformationworld.com/2026/03/creator-economy-report-515-of-creators.html)).
- Small accounts (<5K): ~43 views per 100 followers ([AdManage.ai](https://admanage.ai/blog/average-tiktok-views)).
- Averages are far higher than medians because of viral outliers: Metricool 2026 reports 11,429 average views for the 2K–10K tier ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/short-video-social-media-engagement-benchmarks-2026/)); TikTok average views per post fell 31% YoY to 32,895 amid content saturation (2.3M posts) ([Metricool TikTok study 2026](https://metricool.com/press-release-tiktok-study-2026/)); TikTok content volume up ~80% in early 2026 while per-post performance declined ([NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/tiktok-content-volume-up-nearly-80-percent-in-early-2026-as-per-post-performance-declined-report-finds/)).
- **"200-view jail" / "view jail":** creator folklore for a ~200–300 view plateau; no official TikTok documentation. Creators report "most of my videos have gotten 200 to 300 views"; suggested causes are speculative (flags, inconsistent posting, weak resonance) ([stripe.jhu.edu reposted guide](https://stripe.jhu.edu/news/tiktok-nightmare-unlocking-the-secrets-of-200-view-jail)). Treat as unverified folklore; the Heepsy median suggests ~300–600 views is simply normal for sub-10K accounts.
- Anecdotal outlier: an 89-follower fitness page hit 2.3M views on one clip ([Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/viral-tiktok-small-following/)).

### Instagram Reels
- **Accounts with 1K–5K followers average a 9.78% Reel reach rate** (140K business Reels, Jan–Jun 2026); carousels 8.80%, images 7%; reach rate falls to 5.00% at 100K–1M ([Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-statistics/)).
- Reels of **30–60 s generate the highest reach rate (5.60% avg)**; >2 min falls to 3.50%; a separate analysis found 45–60 s Reels produce highest engagement and median views ([Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/Instagram-reels-stats)).
- Older Metricool-derived absolute figures: 501–2,000 followers ≈ 1,200 views per Reel; 2,001–10,000 ≈ 2,900 (date unclear) ([Blogging Wizard](https://bloggingwizard.com/instagram-reels-statistics)).
- Accounts under 10K followers average ~3.8% engagement vs 1.1% over 1M (source chain unclear) ([Zelios](https://zelios.agency/instagram-reels-statistics/)).
- **Only 21% of accounts under 10K followers grew in 2026** ([Metricool Instagram study 2026](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/)).
- Brands saw ~200% YoY follower growth on TikTok in 2025 while IG organic growth slowed (Emplifi via [Mekanism](https://superstudio.mekanism.com/cult-dump/tiktok-dominates-brand-growth-as-instagrams-organic-reach-continues-to-fade)) — argues for TikTok-first.
- Cross-platform: TikTok leads in views for tiny-through-big accounts; Instagram leads in shares at every size and in comments in most tiers (375,462 accounts) ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/short-video-social-media-engagement-benchmarks-2026/)).

### Best posting times (local audience time)
- **Sprout Social 2026 (≈2B engagements, 307K profiles):** Instagram best windows Mon 2–4 pm, Tue 1–7 pm, Wed 12–9 pm, Thu 12–2 pm; weekends lowest; Tue/Wed highest peaks ([Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-instagram/)). TikTok: Tue–Thu, 2–6 pm per the same report as summarized by [eclincher](https://www.eclincher.com/articles/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok-in-2026-what-the-data-really-says).
- **Buffer 2026 (7.1M TikTok posts):** weekends best; Sunday 9 am, Mon 1 pm, Sun 1 pm; Saturday best day overall; Buffer's page also lists Monday 7 pm/6 pm/5 pm peaks — figures conflict, check the original ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok)).
- **Hootsuite 2025 (1M+ posts, 118 countries):** Thu 6–9 am and Sat 10 am–6 pm ([Hopper HQ summary](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok/)).
- Consensus from aggregators: "timing matters less on TikTok than any other platform"; test 6–9 am and 4–9 pm bands and use TikTok's own Follower Activity analytics ([Neal Schaffer](https://nealschaffer.com/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok/)). Note Bermuda is AST (UTC-4 / UTC-3 DST) — one hour ahead of US Eastern; schedule to US Eastern since that is the buying audience.

### Ideal length
- TikTok completion falls with length: ~89% for 7-s clips vs ~8% for 10-min; **15 s is the best completion/watch-time balance** (10.9 s avg watch time) — labeled "representative 2026 benchmark estimates" ([TTCalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/completion-rate-by-video-length/)).
- FanPageKarma: engagement comes from **medium-length videos, not shortest/longest; reach drops significantly past 90 s** ([FanPageKarma](https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/optimal-tiktok-video-length/)).
- Opus.pro argues for a ~41-second sweet spot in 2026 ([Opus](https://www.opus.pro/blog/how-long-should-a-tiktok-be-2026)); talking-head/educational: 30–90 s ([Stack Influence](https://stackinfluence.com/blog/best-tiktok-video-lengths-for-creators-in-2026)).
- Practical rule for DigiRoo: 7–15 s product-demo loops (rise/tilt in one motion) + 30–60 s founder/expert explainers.

### Cadence
- Physio-marketing guides recommend starting at **2 Reels/week, moving to 3 only if sustainable** ([Physitrack](https://www.physitrack.com/insights/how-to-grow-instagram-physical-therapist)). Quso.ai has a posting-frequency study ([Quso.ai](https://quso.ai/research/how-often-to-post-on-tiktok)) — figures not in snippet (**unverified**).
- Reels posting cadence grew 3.8% from 2024 to 2025 while image posts fell 6.41% ([Neal Schaffer](https://nealschaffer.com/how-much-does-an-influencer-make/)).

### Creator pay benchmarks (for budgeting collabs)
- Instagram per-post: Nano (1K–10K) $10–$100; Mega (1M+) $10,000+ ([Neal Schaffer](https://nealschaffer.com/how-much-does-an-influencer-make/)).
- TikTok Shop affiliate: US average commission ~13.02%; ranges 5–30% per SKU; electronics ~5%; commissions paid after return window ([Hamster Garage](https://www.hamstergarage.com/article/tiktok-shop-affiliate-commission-rates-fees-payouts); [Dashboardly](https://dashboardly.io/post/tiktok-shop-affiliate-commissions-2026-payouts-clawbacks-profit-math)).

## 6. Implications for DigiRoo's content (short)
1. DigiRoo's ~500 views is above the sub-10K TikTok median (378); the problem is conversion and volume, not "view jail." Publish 3–5x/week of 7–15 s demo loops plus 1–2 explainers weekly to generate enough shots at the 1-in-6 that clears 1K.
2. Copy the PT "one simple fix" structure: hook ("your kid's iPad is 4 inches too low"), demo rise/tilt in one motion, end with a question to farm comments.
3. Seed product to the pediatric PT/chiro mid-tier (@ptandparenthood, @theptmama, @kelseymcdonald.pt, @just4kidsphysio, @kidsonlychiropractic) and mom-tech tutorial creators (@momtestedfinds) rather than paying mega-accounts; offer a 15–20% TikTok Shop open-collab commission.
4. Post Tue–Thu afternoons (Sprout) and test Sat/Sun mornings (Buffer); measure in US Eastern.
5. Use the #techneck cluster + #ipadkids + #pediatricpt tags and write captions as search queries; add #creatorsearchinsights.

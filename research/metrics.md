# Metrics plan (D11)

## What we measure, where it comes from, and the target

| Metric | Source | Baseline (Oct 2026) | 3-week target | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Posts published / week (TT + IG + FB) | weekly-calendar.md status column | ~0–2 | 5 (+FB mirror) | Consistency is the first lever (H1) |
| Median views per post (TikTok) | TikTok Studio analytics | ~500 | >600 (out of view jail), then >1,500 | Reach |
| Median views per post (Reels) | IG Insights | unknown (collect this week) | >600 | Reach |
| Saves + shares per post | TT Studio / IG Insights | unknown | ≥10 on best post/week | Shareability (H2) |
| Avg watch time / retention at 3 s | TT Studio | unknown | ≥60% past 3 s | Hook quality |
| Followers (TT, IG) | profiles | collect this week | +100 / +100 | Organic base for retargeting |
| Profile visits → link clicks | IG Insights, Linktree/Shopify UTM | unknown | 2% of views | Intent |
| Shopify sessions / orders / conversion | Shopify Analytics | ~3 orders/month | 8 orders/month | Revenue |
| Discount-code uses by affiliate | Shopify Discounts | 0 | first affiliate sale | Affiliate channel works |
| Clinicians contacted / replied / onboarded / posted | affiliates/tracker.csv | 1–2 onboarded | 30 / 8 / 4 / 2 | H3 |
| Email captures | Shopify / Klaviyo (if used) | unknown | 20 | Owned audience |
| Antonia hours per week on content | timer in weekly-review | unknown (est. 4–6 h, inconsistent) | ≤2 h | H4 |

## Collection routine
- **Monday, 10 min:** Antonia screenshots TT Studio (last 7 days, per post) + IG Insights (per Reel) + Shopify Analytics overview, drops them in the Claude Project, says "review the week".
- `weekly-review` fills the per-post table and appends to `lessons-learned.md`.
- Baseline capture this week: last 10 posts on each account (views, likes, comments, shares, saves, date, format). Done, wider: TikTok all 30 posts, Instagram last 20 own posts (`research/baseline-tiktok.md`, `research/baseline-instagram.md`).

## Analysis cuts for the paper
- Views by pillar, by format, by hook type, by time slot, kid-on-camera vs. not.
- Before/after: median views for the 10 posts before Week 01 vs. Weeks 01–03.
- Affiliate funnel conversion at each step; time-to-reply by channel (DM vs. email).
- Time study: minutes per step (plan, shoot, cut, schedule) across Weeks 01–03.

# Personas and empathy maps (D11)

## External persona 1 — "Worried Mom" (Dana, 38, Boston suburb; or Keisha, 35, Hamilton, Bermuda)
**Snapshot:** two kids (6 and 9), both use an iPad daily (shows, Roblox, school apps). Works part-time or full-time; phone is her shopping and scrolling device, 9–11 pm. Follows mom creators, a pediatrician or two, and a chiropractor who posts "posture checks".

| Says | Thinks |
|---|---|
| "Sit up, you're going to hurt your neck." (x15/day) | "Am I a bad parent for letting them have this much screen time?" |
| "I'll look at it later." | "If a product solved one of the fights, I'd pay $50 without thinking." |

| Does | Feels |
|---|---|
| Saves reels she means to come back to; buys from TikTok Shop / Amazon / brand sites after seeing a product 3+ times | Guilty, tired, protective, a little defensive when lectured |

**Pains:** nagging doesn't work; guilt; too many "shoulds"; doesn't know what's actually harmful vs. fine.
**Gains:** one less fight; something visible she did for their health; a product that looks good in the living room.
**Jobs to be done:** make screen time less bad without removing it; feel like a good parent; avoid conflict.
**Where she sees us:** TikTok FYP, IG Reels via a clinician she follows, a mom friend's share, Amazon search "kids ipad case stand".
**What convinces her:** the side-profile before/after; another mom's kid using it; a clinician saying "this is what I'd use"; price under $50; fits her iPad.

## External persona 2 — "Pediatric Chiro / PT" (Dr. Maya, 34, family chiropractic clinic, Austin; or a physio at a Bermuda clinic)
**Snapshot:** ICPA-trained, sees 15–25 kids/week, posts 3×/week on IG (adjustment clips, posture tips, "tech neck" reminders), 2–15k followers, TikTok sometimes. Runs the clinic's socials herself or with a front-desk person.

| Says | Thinks |
|---|---|
| "Screens aren't going away; we work with what's real." | "I need content every week and I'm out of ideas." |
| "I don't endorse products." | "…unless it's genuinely useful and doesn't make me look salesy." |

| Does | Feels |
|---|---|
| Recommends pillows, backpacks, desks by brand already; hands out printed tip sheets | Protective of her credibility; happy to help parents; busy |

**Pains:** time to create content; liability worries; parents asking "so what do I buy?" with no good answer.
**Gains:** ready-made posts; something concrete to recommend; small revenue; looks current.
**Jobs:** educate parents; fill the content calendar; add a passive revenue line without feeling like an influencer.
**What convinces her:** free unit, no medical claims, clean code, 3 posts she can use as-is, Antonia's founder credibility (patent, Fast Company).
**What loses her:** cold mass DMs, "doctor-recommended" language, having to film anything.

## Internal persona — Antonia (founder, ~2 days/week)
**Snapshot:** inventor, mom, Bermuda-based, ~1,200 units sold, DTC on Shopify, uses Claude (financials), ChatGPT (creative), Canva, Drive, Meta Business Suite. Has units, a daughter, an iPhone.

| Says | Thinks |
|---|---|
| "I have the ideas. I don't have the time to shoot, edit and post." | "Every week I don't post, the account dies a little." |
| "Paid ads didn't work." | "Because there's no organic base to retarget. I know." |

| Does | Feels |
|---|---|
| Posts in bursts; saves reels; keeps a Drive of UGC; answers DMs | Capable, stretched, slightly discouraged by 500-view posts, hopeful about affiliates |

**Pains:** time to get media, time to edit, time to post; view jail; affiliates not converting.
**Gains:** a repeatable 2-hour week; posts that compound; clinicians recommending it; the account becoming the retargeting base for ads later.
**Jobs:** keep DigiRoo growing on 2 days/week; prove the channel before hiring help.
**Success looks like:** Monday 20-min brain dump → one 60–90-min shoot → cuts and queue done by Claude → she says "go".

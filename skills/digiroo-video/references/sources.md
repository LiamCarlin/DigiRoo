# Sources — where each technique in this skill came from

All eight source skills are MIT-licensed (© 2026 their authors). Nothing was copied wholesale: this skill re-implements their rules and numbers in its own scripts and adapts them from code-rendered or screen-recorded video to real phone footage. Studied 2026-11-04 from each repo's default branch.

| Skill | Repo | What DigiRoo took |
|---|---|---|
| **Take One** (Tal Siach) | github.com/talsiach/take-one | 9:16 safe zone (x 56–940, y 250/285–1480, rail beyond x 940 from y 880); type floors (≥70 px read, headline gets a second line not a smaller size); 2–5 words a caption, no full stop; minimum hold 0.4 s + 0.2 s/word; voice chain (high-pass 85, +2 dB @3.2 k, −1.5 dB @230, 3:1 compressor); −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP; adaptive speech detection (p95 − 30 dB); "fast actions, slow meanings"; zoom limits (warn >1.12×); contact sheet + review pack; fresh-reviewer loop, 8/10 bar, "makers grade ~2 points high"; TASTE.md → `video-notes.md` |
| **video-director** (win4r) | github.com/win4r/video-director | Brief → plan → build → verify loop; "frame 0 is a finished composition"; hook ≥84 px by 3 s; holds max(1.5 s, 1 s + 0.3 s/word); ≤3.5 words/s; picture follows voice (cut ~0.3 s before the next line); vignette arc 25/45/30; render QC (frame-one stats, flash, frozen ≥1 s, cut detection); loudness measured on the MP4 with ebur128; three review layers, critic "default to reject", one fix round; "the user should never be the first viewer"; DECISION log |
| **explainroo** (Vincent Schmalbach) | github.com/vincentsch/explainroo | Every key optional with sensible defaults; spoken words are the timeline; captions ≤28 chars breaking at phrase ends; don't show on screen the sentence the captions already show; the first frame is the cover (title never animates in); vertical safe-area fractions; blackdetect/silencedetect verify |
| **huashu-motion** (Huashu) | github.com/alchaincyf/huashu-motion | Phone footage is HDR → convert to SDR first; give Whisper the script as spoken; restraint (one element per ~4 s); text lands just ahead of the word; product-demo continuity (before/after framed identically; keep the in-between state); music ~12 LU under voice, ducked under every word; calm acoustic 80–110 BPM for family brands; let the footage carry the colour, neutral type; one plain message with defaults instead of a question form; taste memory in the user's words, dated; Poppins (OFL) |
| **rossark-motion-skill** (rossark0) | github.com/rossark0/rossark-motion-skill | Text arrives from 0.9–0.97 scale with opacity, ease-out (0.23, 1, 0.32, 1), no bounce, never from 0; nothing under ~100 ms at 30 fps (it flickers); exits faster than entrances; never transition through an empty frame |
| **earshot** (Leon Lin) | github.com/Leonxlnx/earshot | Every sound has a visible cause (the stand's click is the payoff sound); restraint: no sound on hard cuts, ≤2 designed sounds a short video; music stop-down 6–12 dB for 0.3–0.5 s before the hit; SFX placed by peak, ±45/+75 ms sync window; licence rules (CC0 / Mixkit / Pixabay, no NC/ND, read the licence on the item's page); limiter ceiling ≈ −2 dBFS so AAC stays under −1 dBTP; visual-event detector (96×54 grey frame difference) → `find_lift`; "never loosen a threshold to pass"; evidence rule |
| **argo** (Shreyas Karnik) | github.com/shreyaskarnik/argo | Export: libx264 (not VideoToolbox: banding), `aq-mode=3:aq-strength=0.8:deblock=1,1`, BT.709 tags, AAC 192 k, 90 kHz timescale; `amix normalize=0`; camera language (zoompan push-ins, not crop); blur-fill for landscape as a fallback only; "one overlay per zone"; symptom → cause → fix tables |
| **reanimate** (Niaz Morshed) | github.com/NiazMorshed2007/reanimate | The analyse → rebuild → verify loop, adapted to "make it like this reel": a beat table (cuts, shot lengths, biggest move, loudness) compared to ours, side-by-side sheets; fix one cause per round; don't quietly lower the threshold |

What none of them covered, written for DigiRoo from the niche research in `research/segment-analysis.md` and standard iPhone practice: the phone setup, the one-take slouch-to-lift, directing a child on camera and her privacy, and the clip triage (`check_clips.py`).

# Screen posture at home — a one-page handout from {{CLINIC}}

*Four small habits for kids who use tablets and phones. Not medical advice; if your child has neck or back pain, book a visit.*

## 1. Eyes up, not screen down
The lower the screen, the further the head drops forward. Bring the screen up to your child's eye level: a stand, a stack of books, a case with a stand built in. If the screen is in their lap, their head is in their lap.

## 2. The 20-minute reset
Every 20 minutes: stand up, look at something far away for 20 seconds, roll the shoulders back twice. Make it a game: pause the show, race to touch the far wall.

## 3. Sit back, not forward
Bottom to the back of the seat, feet on the floor or a step, screen in front (not to the side). On the couch, a cushion behind the lower back helps smaller kids.

## 4. Same rules for the car and the table
Car: screen on a stand or mounted at seat height, not held in the lap. Table: elbows on the table, screen propped up. Floor time: lying on the tummy with the screen propped up beats sitting hunched.

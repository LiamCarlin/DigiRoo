## The problem you already talk about
Kids hunch over tablets. You explain it to parents every week: the lower the screen, the further the head drops forward, the more load on a growing neck. Parents nod, go home, and the iPad goes right back on their kid's lap.

## What DigiRoo is
A protective kids' iPad case with a patented, height-adjustable stand built in. It lifts the screen up to 4.4 inches and tilts up to 60°, with 25+ positions, so the screen comes up to the child's eyes instead of the child's neck going down to the screen. BPA-free, drop-friendly, fits iPad 10.2" (7th–9th gen; 10th-gen version available). Fast Company Innovation by Design 2022, Honourable Mention. Designed by a Bermuda mom, Antonia Holder.

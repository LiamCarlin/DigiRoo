## 3. Who we're talking to (personas)

### P1 — "Worried Mom" (primary buyer)
- Mom (sometimes dad or grandparent) of a 4–12-year-old who uses an iPad daily for shows, games, school apps. US + Bermuda. Shops on phone, late evening.
- **What she feels:** guilt about screen time, worry about her kid's neck/eyes/posture, exhaustion from nagging "sit up". Wants a fix that doesn't require a fight.
- **What she notices:** the slouch on the couch, kid lying on the floor face-down over the iPad, the "iPad neck" curl in the car.
- **What makes her buy:** seeing another mom's kid using it; a clinician saying "this is what I'd use"; a quick before/after; a price that feels like one less thing to worry about.
- **What makes her scroll:** medical lectures, hard sells, anything that makes her feel judged.

### P2 — "Pediatric Chiro / PT" (affiliate + amplifier)
- Pediatric or family chiropractor, pediatric physiotherapist, often ICPA-certified, clinic in a US metro or Bermuda. Posts 2–5×/week on IG, some on TikTok; content = posture tips, kid adjustments, "tech neck" warnings, parent education.
- **What they want:** credible, shareable parent-education content; a product they can recommend without liability worry; a little extra revenue; to look current.
- **What makes them say yes:** free unit to use in-clinic, ready-made posts they can repost, a clean code/link, no hard-sell language, zero effort.
- **What makes them say no:** medical claims they'd have to defend, spammy DMs, no clear "what's in it for my patients".

### P3 — Antonia (internal user of this system)
- Solo founder, ~2 days/week on DigiRoo, uses Claude (financials), ChatGPT (creative), Canva, Google Drive, Shopify, Meta Business Suite. Has her daughter, units, and a phone. Her scarce resource is **time**, not ideas.
- Wants: one sitting of planning, one short shoot, posts scheduled, affiliates contacted, without learning a new tool.

## 4. Voice

- **Parent-to-parent.** Warm, direct, a bit wry. Like a friend who happens to have invented the thing.
- **Short sentences. Spoken English.** Contractions. One idea per sentence.
- **Show, don't lecture.** Kid on couch, slouch, lift, done.
- **Banned words:** elevate (as a verb in copy — the tagline is the only exception), game-changer, revolutionary, unlock, seamless, effortlessly, must-have, guaranteed, cure, fix (as in "fixes posture"), prevent (as in "prevents injury"), medical, treatment, therapy, clinically.
- **Good words:** sit up, lift, eye level, hunch, slouch, "tech neck" (as a nickname, not a diagnosis), screen time, comfy, habit, little humans, big screen, "one less thing".

## 5. Claims policy (consumer product, not a medical device)

| ✅ Say | ❌ Don't say |
|---|---|
| "Lifts the screen to eye level so kids aren't hunched over." | "Fixes / corrects / prevents tech neck." |
| "Helps build a sit-up-straight habit." | "Treats posture problems." |
| "Designed with input from parents and posture pros." | "Doctor-recommended" / "clinically proven" (unless we have a named clinician on record). |
| "Looking down at a screen puts a lot of extra load on a kid's neck." | Specific numbers like "40 lb of pressure" unless cited on screen to a source (e.g., Hansraj 2014 estimate for adults — note it's adults). |
| "Pediatric chiropractors use it in their clinics." (once ≥1 affiliate confirms) | Any clinician endorsement before they've agreed in writing. |
